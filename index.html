Dr. Sara Sutton is an Assistant Professor in the College of Computing at Grand Valley State University. She received her Ph.D. from Oakland University in 2020, where her dissertation focused on exploring cyber deception techniques—particularly decoy processes as a defense mechanism in cyber-physical and operating systems.

Before entering academia full-time, she worked as an application developer at an insurance company, where she led the development and maintenance of core backend systems critical to the company’s operations. Her work combined software engineering, data analysis, and system design to solve practical challenges.

Research Interests

Her research is centered on the design and development of resilient cybersecurity solutions using:

  • Cyber deception - proactive defense mechanisms to detect attackers
  • Machine learning - applied to malware detection, anomaly analysis, and exploit resilience
  • Security for cyber-physical systems – including embedded systems and operating systems
  • Cloud and IoT security – addressing vulnerabilities in distributed and resource-constrained environments
  • Drone cybersecurity – securing autonomous and remote drone operations from cyber threats

Selected Grants

Her research and projects have been supported by a combination of federal and institutional grants, including:

  • NASA, MSGC - Anomaly detection system for secure drone operations, 2025, PI
  • NSA, NSF, GVSU Gencyber summer teacher program, 2023–2025, PI
  • GVSU research Seed Grant, Real-time network packet detection for enhanced security in fog computing, 2025 , Co-PI
  • GVSU GA Project, VNF resource defense using deception, 2024–2025 , PI
  • GVSU GA Project, Cyberbullying threat detection research, , 2023–2024, PI
